# Discovery interview guide — no responses recorded

Ask permission to take deidentified notes; separately ask permission before quoting.
Participation and pricing questions are voluntary. Record DECLINED when appropriate,
never infer a price or fabricate an answer. Do not collect names/employer details.

- What decision does your current judge support, and who owns that decision?
- How do you evaluate it today? Which tools, reviewer time and optional spending
  can you describe from actual experience? Distinguish estimates from records.
- Who supplies reference labels? How are disagreements resolved, and at what effort?
- What false accept mattered most, and how was it independently confirmed?
- Could you legally use deidentified or synthetic cases locally for this comparison?
- During this demo, which result can you reproduce and which denominator is unclear?
- What changed in an actual decision? What evidence supports that observation?
- Which finding was new relative to conventional review, and who verified it?
- Which existing tool could do this? What capability or integration is still missing?
- Would you try this again? Under what conditions? Is payment relevant, and for which
  concrete outcome? Stated intent is not usage, revenue or a commitment.
- What would make you stop using it? What privacy/data/review constraints block it?

For every note distinguish OBSERVED, PARTICIPANT_REPORTED and ANALYST_INFERENCE.
Capture time with a timer and actual outputs, not a guess. Confirm interpretation
with the participant. Preserve negative, confusing and zero-value observations.
