# Stage C — Five real-user pilots and a decision

**Execute prompts 09–10.** Human-controlled user recruitment, dataset permission and product discovery are prerequisites.

## Five-user study (not scientific proof of broad market demand)
For each participant, capture current workflow, previous tool/spend, baseline outcome, Judge Lab outcome, verified incremental findings, total reviewer minutes, actual user decision affected, invalid findings, confusion, and desire to reuse. Only humans can provide these facts.

## Definition of completed pilot
Participant consent, relevant role, completion date, actual usage session, properly sourced observations, independently checked findings, deidentified record and explicit permission to quote (if quoted). Mock runs, imaginary personas, website impressions, or a synthetic-only demo are not completed pilot sessions.

## Last gate questions
1. Did at least two people independently use the CLI without your hands on keyboard?
2. Did anyone make a meaningfully different release/review decision because of a **verified** failure?
3. Were reviewers willing and able to supply trustworthy reference labels?
4. Is total cost acceptable compared with current platform + labeled set?
5. Which feature would they pay for, and what existing bill does it replace or complement?
6. Is there an identifiable wedge even if no proprietary AI method is novel?

## Decision options
**Ship narrow local tool / integrate into incumbent / pivot to different buyer/workflow / stop**. Record evidence and opposing facts. Don't put "enterprise scale" or 17-domain expansion on roadmap without repeated paid usage and validated transfer.
